WEARABLE SENSING · 2020–2021

Portable Real-Time Ankle Motion Analysis

Wearable IMU sensing and real-time feedback for ankle-joint motion during gait and running.

Yonsei University 2020–2021

Project

We developed a portable system for measuring ankle-joint motion with wearable IMUs and providing real-time audio-visual feedback outside a traditional motion-capture laboratory.

Representative running trial with wearable IMU feedback.

Additional trial demonstrating repeatable real-time feedback.

Experimental validation setup in motion.

Validation

The wearable measurements were validated against a motion-capture reference during running. This work contributed to a 2021 paper in Sensors on measuring ankle-joint movements using IMUs.

My contribution

I developed hardware and software for the portable feedback system and contributed to the Vicon experiments used for validation.

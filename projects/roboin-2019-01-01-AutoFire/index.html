ROBOTICS · 2019

Autonomous Fire Tracking and Extinguishing Robot

A thermal-camera-guided mobile robot for tracking heat sources and autonomous fire suppression.

Yonsei University · Roboin 2019 Team lead

Project

For a senior project in Yonsei’s robotics club, our team built a mobile robot that used a thermal camera to detect and track heat sources, approach them, and aim an extinguishing mechanism.

Autonomous heat-source tracking and fire-suppression demo.

Mobility / stair test.

Robot communication and control demo.

My contribution

I served as team leader and led the hardware/software integration, including mobile-robot control and the heat-source tracking pipeline.

Recognition

The project received 2nd Prize in the Robot Open Source Lab competition at Samsung Open Source Conference 2019.

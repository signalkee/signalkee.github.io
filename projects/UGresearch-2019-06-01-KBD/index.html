INDUSTRIAL SAFETY · 2019

Smart Lockout/Tagout System

An intelligent safety system for monitoring and controlling locked industrial equipment.

Yonsei University 2019

Project

We developed an intelligent lockout/tagout concept for monitoring the state of locked industrial equipment and improving procedural safety.

Lockout/tagout system demonstration.

Outcome

The work led to a paper at IEEE IEEM 2019 on an intelligent lockout/tagout system for monitoring and control of locked devices.

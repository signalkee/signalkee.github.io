RESEARCH FOCUS

Selective communication, mission-aware reasoning, and prediction-guided execution.

I study how robot teams identify useful information, communicate it selectively, and choose actions using mission context and predicted consequences.

01

Selective communication

Move only useful information through the team: who should communicate, when, and what should be exchanged.

02

Mission-aware reasoning

Connect observations and mission context to the information and decisions that matter for the team.

03

Prediction-guided execution

Compare candidate actions using predicted downstream communication, task, and risk outcomes.

ABOUT

Building toward practical autonomy systems.

I am a Ph.D. student in Robotics at the University of Michigan, working on multi-robot autonomy, communication-aware coordination, and learning-enabled control. My research connects selective communication, mission-aware reasoning, prediction-guided action, and real robotic systems.

I am advised by Prof. Vasileios Tzoumas and Prof. Necmiye Ozay at the University of Michigan. Through the Draper Scholars Program, I am also advised by Dr. Begum Cannataro at Charles Stark Draper Laboratory. Before my Ph.D., I worked on learning-enabled safety-critical control at Michigan and on wearable assistive robots at KIST.

Outside research, I enjoy weightlifting and music.

## Project

We developed a portable system for measuring ankle-joint motion with wearable IMUs and providing real-time audio-visual feedback outside a traditional motion-capture laboratory.

## Validation

The wearable measurements were validated against a motion-capture reference during running. This work contributed to a 2021 paper in _Sensors_ on measuring ankle-joint movements using IMUs.

## My contribution

I developed hardware and software for the portable feedback system and contributed to the Vicon experiments used for validation.

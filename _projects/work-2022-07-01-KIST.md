## Goal

Daily assistive robots can do more than provide torque assistance: their onboard sensing can also reveal how a person is walking. At KIST, I worked on estimating gait trajectories and clinically relevant gait parameters from wearable-robot and insole sensors.

## Learning-based gait estimation

I developed deep-learning models that fuse robot and wearable sensing to estimate foot trajectories and gait parameters for treadmill and overground walking.

## Problem

Adaptive cruise control in dynamic traffic must make progress toward a desired speed while respecting collision-avoidance and actuation constraints around moving vehicles.

## Approach

We combined a **Time Shift Governor**, model predictive control, and **Collision Cone Control Barrier Functions** so that reference adaptation and short-horizon control work together when the nominal command would approach unsafe or infeasible behavior.

## Why it matters

The project explores how reference-governor ideas and barrier-function constraints can complement predictive control in environments where obstacles move and the safe action changes continuously.

## My contribution

I co-developed the formulation and implementation and contributed to the simulation study and paper.

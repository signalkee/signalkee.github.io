## Problem

In-vehicle acoustic classifiers must recognize diverse noise sources while adapting to limited data and new classes.

## Work

I worked on few-shot and continual-learning approaches for in-vehicle noise classification, including prototype-based representation learning and replay strategies.

## Outcomes

The work led to an IEEE Access paper on disentangled prototyping with a triplet-trained prototypical network and a KDMS paper on dynamic prototype-guided memory replay.
